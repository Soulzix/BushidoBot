💰 Currency & Economy Commands (Everyone)
/balance – Check your current yen.
/daily – Claim your daily yen reward.
/weekly – Collect your weekly bonus.
/work – Earn yen through work. (Cooldown: 1 hour)
/beg – Ask for yen (chance of failure). (Cooldown: 10 minutes)
/gamble (amount) – Bet yen for a chance to win or lose.
/rob @user – Attempt to steal yen. (Chance of failure & jail time!)
/deposit (amount) – Store yen in your bank.
/withdraw (amount) – Take yen out of the bank.
🛒 Shop System (Everyone)
/shop – Opens the shop menu (starts at Page 1).
◀️ Previous – Goes back one shop page.
▶️ Next – Moves forward one shop page.
/buy (item name) – Purchases an item.
/sell (item name) – Sells an item.
/inventory – Shows your owned items.
🔹 General Commands (Everyone)
/wakeup – Forces the bot to respond if it's idle or unresponsive.
/help – Displays all available commands.
/rules – Shows server rules.
/ping – Checks bot response time.
/serverinfo – Displays server details.
/userinfo @user – Shows information about a user.
/avatar @user – Retrieves a user’s avatar.
/invite – Generates an invite link.
/suggest (suggestion) – Sends a suggestion to staff.
/report @user (reason) – Reports a user.
/poll (question) – Starts a poll.
/verify – Grants server access (via role, captcha, or approval).
/connectroblox (Roblox Username) – Links your Roblox account to Discord.
🔹 In-Game Profile Management (Linked Roblox Account Required)
/changename (new in-game name) – Changes your in-game character name.
/changeavatar (new avatar image URL) – Changes your in-game character avatar.
/changeoutfit (outfit ID) – Changes your in-game outfit by providing the Roblox outfit ID.
🔧 Staff-Only Commands (Moderators & Admins)
/mute @user (time) – Mutes a user for a set time.
/unmute @user – Unmutes a user.
/ban @user (reason) – Bans a user.
/unban @user – Unbans a user.
/kick @user (reason) – Kicks a user.
/warn @user (reason) – Issues a warning.
/clear (amount) – Deletes a number of messages.
/slowmode (time) – Enables slow mode in a channel.
/lock – Locks a channel for everyone.
/unlock – Unlocks a channel.
/rulebook – Displays server rules (for mods+ only).
